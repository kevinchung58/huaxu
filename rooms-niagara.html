The falls are drawn from public facts — the crest's breadth, the drop, the talus, the islands, the rainbow's habit, the nightly illumination — and simplified at the detail level only: no crowds, no lettering, the downstream skyline a silhouette, no claim that the owner stood at the rail. The mist is the weather the sources describe.
