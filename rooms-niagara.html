The place is drawn from the map and the sources: the trail, the terrace and Table Rock Centre's footprint, the Horseshoe's brink, the American and Bridal Veil falls, Goat and Luna Islands and the gorge's edges are OpenStreetMap's; the 57 m drop, the American Falls' 21-30 m onto its boulders, the Skylon's 160 m and the bridge's 290 m arch are the sources'. Approximate and said so: the rim of the gorge north of the terrace (drawn 30-40 m inland of the mapped water), the cliff's split between rock face and scree, the falling water's 24 m throw, the woods' height on the islands, Table Rock Centre's 10 m and its plain faces, the lamp posts' places. No crowds, no boats on the water, no lettering, and no claim that the owner stood at the rail. The Parks line downstream -- White Water Walk, the Aero Car, the Floral Clock, the Glen -- and the Fallsview towers up the hill stay in the drawer.
