With a finger: drag to turn, tap the wall in front of you to open it, tap again to put it down. Three ways out — the arrow in the corner, the curtain at the mouth of the lane, and a second Esc once nothing is open any more.

The place is drawn from the map and the sources: the trail, the terrace and Table Rock Centre's footprint and two storeys, the Horseshoe's brink, the American and Bridal Veil falls, Goat and Luna Islands and the gorge's edges are OpenStreetMap's; the 57 m drop, the American Falls' 21-30 m onto its boulders, the bridge's 290 m arch, the Skylon's 160 m from a street 76 m over the lower river, the bluff's 30 m rise (the Falls Incline Railway's 59.8 m of track at 30 degrees) and the ten Fallsview hotels' places, heights and floors (Wikipedia's list of the city's tallest buildings) are the sources'. Approximate and said so: the rim of the gorge north of the terrace (drawn 30-40 m inland of the mapped water) and its 38 m of sheer rock above the talus, the falling water's 24 m throw and the spray's height, the woods' height on the islands and the bluff, the bluff's line away from the railway, Table Rock Centre's 9.5 m eaves, its roofs and its cupola's place (read from photographs), the hotels' footprints (30 m; the Tower Hotel's shaft 16 m) and their cladding, the parapet's measures (wall 60 cm, ironwork to 90 cm, posts 96 cm every 3 m) and its iron's pattern, read from photographs at the brink, the lamp posts' places. Left out and said so: the Sky Screamer (a ride 2 km south), the Casino and Rainbow towers and the Sheraton on the Falls down by the river (forms unsourced), and the far shores past two kilometres (under two pixels tall from the rail). No crowds, no boats on the water, no lettering, and no claim that the owner stood at the rail. The Parks line downstream -- White Water Walk, the Aero Car, the Floral Clock, the Glen -- stays in the drawer. Under the shed at the journey door's hand the lane keeps a game: a raft and a bell, the mist's own noise answered in a toy — the shed is drawn, the bell is not surveyed. Round 52 added the east end: the Parkway, Clifton's fun row, the SkyWheel at its far end, and the Falls Incline. The wheel's measures are the manufacturer's sheet; the strip's 500 m of street are the lane's 155, the bluff's 30 m are kept as 5.5, and the incline's 62-second crossing three when you ride it — each compression stated by the thing compressed. The arcade fronts carry no shop: a drawing may keep an arcade's glow, not its lease.
