Moji Port in a model hall. The four historic buildings stand on their own stands at 1:25, south-west of the basin on the left and north-east on the right; their mapped lengths follow OpenStreetMap, while heights and parts read from photographs are approximate. The Retro High Mart is a separate 1:25 model on a lower 30 cm display stand so its 127 m source-listed top fits below the hall ceiling. Its six-node OSM outline (way 292429354) measures about 32.17 × 31.37 m by the table projection, with a mapped polygon area of about 647 m². The 127 m nominal top, including the rooftop helipad, is listed by CVU/CTBUH and corroborated by the building's Wikipedia article and an AIJ paper; the OSM way's height=115 tag has no traceable origin and was not adopted, and no source gives a roof-slab height. Kitakyushu City and MLIT describe the public observation view at 103 m, not the total building height. The City's 755.45 m² exclusive area and 498.38 m² observation-room area are floor areas, not footprint; the building's registered building area of about 830 m² (803.56 m² in the AIJ table) is a filed maximum horizontal section — a different measurement from the mapped outline — so the model keeps the OSM ring and no primary footprint plan was found. The tower's light pier-and-spandrel grid over dark teal glass, dark X-braced transfer band, open crown frame and curved cap follow the sourced Nippon.com photograph; exact floor spacing and roof geometry remain approximate. The port table is laid out from OpenStreetMap at 1.5 cm to the metre, read from the head of the No. 1 basin looking north-west, with all five outlines as footprints; the strait beyond the far wall is the same scale. The Mitsui Club, the hotel and Kaikyo Plaza are not modelled because reliable shape and height data remain insufficient. The Blue Wing keeps its real timetable, so it is shut after dark. No sign carries lettering and the yatai sells nothing named. Nothing here says the owner was in any of these places.
