The LookOut is drawn from public facts: 346 m, the window wall's 12 bays of 1.61 m by 2.62 m panes, the glass floor of 2017 above the 1994 original, the SkyPod's own lift. The city below is the map's and the record's: the shore, the islands and the spit from OpenStreetMap, and every tower of 150 m and over -- all 117 on Wikipedia's list -- at its coordinates and height. Approximate and said so: the ring's curve straightened into one chord, the walk's 5.6 m width, the towers' footprints, the glass floor's size, the shore east of Sugar Beach, the runway's line, the towers' storeys (a typical office or residential floor, not each building's own), the ground (drawn level, though the city climbs away from the lake), and the lights between the towers, which are a lit city's density rather than its streets. Missing and said so: the city's nearly three hundred towers between 100 and 150 m, which the list does not place, so below the tall towers the city is lights, not buildings. Nothing here carries a name or a claim that the owner stood at 346 m.
