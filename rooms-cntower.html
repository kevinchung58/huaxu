The LookOut is drawn from public facts: 346 m, the window wall's 12 bays of 1.61 m by 2.62 m panes, the glass floor of 2017 above the 1994 original, the SkyPod's own lift. The city below uses OpenStreetMap shore and island geometry, all 117 listed 150 m+ towers at their published coordinates and heights, and 390 anonymous City massing polygons with AVG_HEIGHT from 100 to under 150 m within 15 km. The latter are source polygons, not 390 distinct buildings: parts near a listed tower are left out to avoid doubling its proxy; footprints under 100 m² are omitted; remaining outlines are simplified by 1.5 m and extruded to the field named AVG_HEIGHT (displayed by the source map in metres); its definition is not independently verified, so it is an approximate extrusion height here, not a surveyed roof maximum. The City supplies the model for illustrative use and disclaims completeness, accuracy and fitness for precision work. Other approximations remain: the LookOut ring is one straight chord, tower footprints are typical squares, the Glass Floor's outline is approximate, the ground is level, and lights between buildings are a city's density rather than surveyed streets or windows. Nothing here names an unverified building or claims that the owner stood at 346 m.
