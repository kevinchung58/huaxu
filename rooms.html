Nakamise-dori as it stands after closing, built from public sources -- Senso-ji's own guide, OpenStreetMap's footprints and the street's published measurements -- with night photographs read for light and material. The 250 m of shops walk in 30 m: the street's order, its width, each shop's width and both gates' sizes are kept, and the count of shops is what shrinks. The shutters keep only the ground colours of the Asakusa Emaki, whose scenes show people; no fascia, lantern or sign carries lettering. The plates on the shutters are drawn. Nothing here says the owner was in this place.
