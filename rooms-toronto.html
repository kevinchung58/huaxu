Building the lane…

WASD walk · Shift run · Space jump · drag to turn · E open a thing · L the list · I this note · Esc close, then leave

With a finger: drag to turn; when something is in front of you, its name appears in the bar at the foot of the picture, and tapping that bar opens it. A tap on the picture itself only puts down what is open. Three ways out — the arrow in the corner, the curtain at the mouth of the lane, and a second Esc once nothing is open any more.

Toronto is drawn, not surveyed: the distances are the artist's, and a frame is a depiction of a place rather than a record of standing in it.

A model hall in the manner of Little Canada, its tables laid out by the real bearings: the city at the far end with the lake in front of it, the market to its east, the falls across the lake to the south-east. On the city table every height keeps the tower's scale (1:184) and the ground between the towers is brought in; the market is 1:100 and its roof height approximate; only towers with sourced heights stand on the skyline. Nothing on any table carries a name, a date or a claim that the owner stood in Toronto.
