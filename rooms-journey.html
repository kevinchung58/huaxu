Behind the falls is drawn, not surveyed: the distances are the artist's, and a frame is a depiction of a place rather than a record of standing in it.

Journey Behind the Falls is drawn from public facts: the 38 m descent, the 1944 concrete-lined tunnel at its 2.4 m section, the main tunnel's 56 m to the deck, the portal branch at 17 m with its portals at 61 m and 110 m (falzguy.com's measured figures), the deck on the cliff, the nightly illumination. Approximate and said so: the tunnel's bearing and the deck's exact place are read from the deck's photographs and the tunnel's length, not surveyed; the section's 2.6 m crown, the boards underfoot and the pipe along the wall are read from visitors' photographs of the tunnels (Niagara Tours, mylifeisajourney.com); the branch is drawn straight; the spray over the basin and the foam's reach are drawn to the photographs, not measured. No crowds, no ponchos, no lettering, and no claim that the owner stood on the deck.
