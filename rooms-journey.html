Building the lane…

WASD walk · Shift run · Space jump · drag to turn · E open a thing · L the list · I this note · Esc close, then leave

— four steps, taught where they are used.

With a finger: drag to turn, tap the wall in front of you to open it, tap again to put it down. Three ways out — the arrow in the corner, the curtain at the mouth of the lane, and a second Esc once nothing is open any more.

Journey Behind the Falls is drawn from public facts: the 38 m descent, the 1944 concrete-lined tunnel at the 2.4 m by about 2 m section its records give it, the main tunnel's 56 m to the deck, the portal branch at 17 m with its portals at 61 m and 110 m (falzguy.com's measured figures; the two tunnels' roughly 46 m behind the falls and their section are also the figures Wikipedia gives, and the lower platform dates to 1951), the deck on the cliff, the nightly illumination. Approximate and said so: the tunnel's bearing and the deck's exact place are read from the deck's photographs and the tunnel's length, not surveyed; the section's 2.6 m crown is that 2 m wall under the barrel's rise, and the boards underfoot and the pipe along the wall are read from visitors' photographs of the tunnels (Niagara Tours, mylifeisajourney.com); the branch is drawn straight; the spray over the basin and the foam's reach are drawn to the photographs, not measured. No crowds, no ponchos, no lettering, and no claim that the owner stood on the deck.
