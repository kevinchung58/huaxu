With a finger: drag to turn, tap the wall in front of you to open it, tap again to put it down. Three ways out — the arrow in the corner, the curtain at the mouth of the lane, and a second Esc once nothing is open any more.

Journey Behind the Falls is drawn from public facts: the 38 m descent, the 1944 concrete-lined tunnel at its 2.4 m section, the main tunnel's 56 m to the deck, the portal branch at 17 m with its portals at 61 m and 110 m (falzguy.com's measured figures), the deck on the cliff, the nightly illumination. Approximate and said so: the tunnel's bearing and the deck's exact place are read from the deck's photographs and the tunnel's length, not surveyed; the branch is drawn straight. No crowds, no ponchos, no lettering, and no claim that the owner stood on the deck.
